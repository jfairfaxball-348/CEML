## Executive decision

**Conditional YES:** a consenting volunteer network could bring the prospective 3×10^21 exclusive verification target into years, potentially one year, but *only* if it supplies hundreds of sustained, independently validating modern-GPU-equivalent workers, not merely hundreds of registered people. Under a deliberately explicit model of **two full independent passes per unit**, 50% hardware duty, and 90% assignment/efficiency factor, about **130 reference-GPU-equivalent installed workers** imply 3 years, **390 imply 1 year**, and ~1,560 imply three months. These are required *effective-equivalent resources under an unverified five-second benchmark*, not volunteer recruitment forecasts. An equivalent of 1,000 such workers yields ~142 days on this model. Nothing establishes that those workers, 24/7 infrastructure, algorithms or validation are available to CEML. Recruitment, correctness and sustained validation are the dominant unknowns.

**Recommendation:** prefer a staged **collaboration-first, benchmark-and-certification-first hybrid**: investigate independently reproducing an existing verified unit with separately implemented code, assess compatibility with the current leading external coordinator, and investigate paid/HPC capacity only after hardware-matched benchmark and evidence-backed independent validation. A BOINC network is a credible later option, not a substitute for correctness design. Reject immediate new-public-BOINC deployment and reject claims that mathematical candidate upper 326518028938128165 makes GPU work proportional to 0.0537% of raw scans.

**Volunteer precedents.** PrimeGrid https://www.primegrid.com/ demonstrates contemporary public mathematical BOINC participation, recognitions, multiple subprojects and GPU/CPU variants; numbers, completion time and validation policies of any chosen subproject need its separate current specification before transplantation. GIMPS https://www.mersenne.org/ is a deeper scientific proof-system lesson: its official mathematical page https://www.mersenne.org/various/math.php describes Pietrzak-style PRP proof verification more than 100× faster than normal duplicate work, adopted in 2020, plus separate Lucas–Lehmer checks for final primality. That technique is **not an existing certificate for Collatz's exhaustive arithmetic coverage**. Its correctness benefit is the cheaply verifiable relation between claimed work and algebraic proof, which CEML has NOT established. Historic Collatz Conjecture BOINC project appears **inactive** according to archived community directories and discussion (https://boincsynergy.ca/wiki/Collatz_Conjecture); an old active-project list is not evidence it works in October 2026. **Bitcoin analogy, and the non-transferable bit.** The Bitcoin developer pool-mining guide https://developer.bitcoin.org/devguide/mining.html has a coordinator issuing block templates, miners supplying below-target nonce/hash "shares", and a cheap SHA-256 recomputation to check each share; share difficulty mainly measures probabilistic effort, not full deterministic coverage of a target arithmetic interval. SHA-256 ASICs cannot execute Collatz 128-bit conditional multiplications and general GMP branches. Mining pools do not provide a Collatz-to-one proof, an enumeration manifest, trusted mathematical validator or monetary rewards; building a token would add expense and incentives to cheat. An unfakeable Collatz analogue of GIMPS's succinct proof is an OPEN design goal rather than a found result. If none exists, two *independent full computations* per unit, adversarial replay and a transparent accepted ledger are the honest provisional architecture.

## 7. Future distributed protocol: exact coverage, trust and failure handling

**Do not retrofit CEML-FRONTIER-2.** Draft a separately versioned research identity for a prospective coordinator. Canonical unit i: start=max(B, i×2^40), end=min(E,(i+1)×2^40), with i from 2175982616 to the last intersecting ID; first unit aligns exactly at B. Alternatively use local index j: [B+j×2^40,min(E,B+(j+1)×2^40)), j=0,…,552501489. A unit with end<=start is illegal. Hash a canonical manifest containing exact decimal start/end, parent manifest root, algorithm and sieve proof versions, intended output/exception schema and owner-selected baseline provenance. Append-only coordinator ledger tracks work-unit identity separate from per-attempt lease. Expiry/reassignment MAY create overlapping attempts, but only one **canonical accepted** record per unit may count toward global contiguous coverage. A database uniqueness constraint on unit ID and transactionally committed (evidence, acceptance, parent/previous root) handles races; publish periodic signed, backed-up snapshot and independent replay. Never mistake "one accepted record per ID" for "exactly one physical execution".

**Validation stages:** (1) syntax/range/build/provenance checking (2) two outputs from independent algorithm implementations on genuinely unrelated hosts, preferably mixed vendors (3) deterministic equality of per-unit mathematical digests and exception/overflow disclosures (4) trusted arbitrator replay on disagreement, and both divergent outputs archived (5) random concealed full replays, plus independent signed trusted repeated units as calibration (6) aggregate unit-index coverage and frontier-prefix proof via the sorted ID set. Replica agreement can miss common code errors, shared bad sieve theory and Sybil collusion; checksum equality alone proves none of the enumerated starts was visited. Random 1% spot-check misses a malicious single bad unit with probability ~0.99 if sampling that unit once (more generally for f fixed bad units out of M and s sample draws, miss probability at most (1−f/M)^s under uniform replacement); spot checks cannot prove exhaustive absence of malicious omission. Even 100% 2× algorithmically independent replay is computational corroboration rather than a proof that independently unverified base A(B) holds. A proof-carrying compressed transcript would be a breakthrough only after a separately proved verifier theorem and benchmark showing transcript verification genuinely cheaper than full replay.

**Threat matrix / HALT:** forged completion or hash → no quorum, trusted replay; checksum collision or duplicated flawed program → require algorithm diversity and independent auditable inputs; Sybil double-host identity → assignment segregation, no identity-only quorum, escrowed/controlled validator lane; nondeterministic CPU/GPU behavior → exact integer semantics with per-version golden vectors and hardware diversity; overflow → explicit exception queue, exact CPU GMP fallback or HALT; last partial unit truncated → manifest exact end, independently check count and final endpoint; expired lease duplicate → atomic canonical-once acceptance, preserve attempt log; source poisoned / stale build → binary signature, reproducible hash and software bill; stale external B → do not silently inherit latest website figures, separate provenance decision; coordinator loss/corrupt DB → append-only hashes, multiple backups and independent reconstruction, HALT on any gap. Any unsolved mismatch yields **NO CLAIM**, not implicit convergence.

**Throughput and storage:** at five seconds/reference unit, 1/10/100/1000/10000 ideal continuously active GPUs generate 0.2/2/20/200/2000 raw completion reports/sec for single-pass dispatch, before replication and efficiency. At duty=.5 and m=2, raw reports halve, and scientifically accepted distinct unit throughput can be ~0.05/0.5/5/50/500 unit IDs/s before e=.9 (then 0.045/0.45/4.5/45/450). 552501490 unit IDs each with two submissions implies ~1.105 billion attempt records. At a hypothetical 1KiB per record, ~1.13TB base reporting, plus backups, logs, data retention, version manifests and exceptional replay; at 200B per record ~221GB. A cryptographic receipt for completion is only a commitment to data. Five-second microtasks imply excessive scheduler overhead for BOINC at scale; propose signed 100–1000-unit bundles with atomic subunit receipts and checkpoints, explicit partial-end handling, retries that do NOT skip subunits, and bounded lease terms. For 1000 reference worker devices ideal rate is 200 submissions/sec, reduced if bundled. CPU jobs may take minutes/hours per 2^40 unit and need distinct lease durations. A hidden frontier hole forces the global frontier to remain at the first missing accepted unit regardless of completed later units.
